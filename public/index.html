This is a working prototype of the EOLPC Knowledge Intelligence System — a searchable AI layer built on real research, practitioner knowledge, and legal frameworks. Type any question and it retrieves primary sources live from PubMed, Semantic Scholar, Europe PMC, ClinicalTrials.gov, and Exa. Every result is an actual paper, a registered trial, or a sourced document. Nothing is invented.

The result: a sourced, citable answer in seconds — the kind of grounding a family or clinician would otherwise spend hours tracking down — while questions that call for individual medical judgment are declined and routed to a real practitioner instead.

How it works

Five-layer RAG architecture

Every response retrieves from five verified data layers, attaches a primary source citation, and routes through a disclaimer check before reaching the user. What the system knows

5-layer knowledge base

Every response retrieves from five verified data layers. Click any layer to see exactly what's in it.

1
Clinical Q&A · 36 In-Depth Zoom Interviews
Unlimited Sciences · EOLPC Psychedelic Death Worker Study · Zak Arrington, University of Chicago
▾

Thirty-six hour-long Zoom interviews with practitioners who hold dual experience in both psychedelic facilitation and end-of-life care — the only population qualified to speak to this intersection. Conducted by Zak Arrington (University of Chicago) in partnership with Unlimited Sciences and EOLPC.

Topics covered: home-based session protocols, managing psychological emergencies in palliative contexts, facilitating acceptance of death, family dynamics during sessions, integration in the context of limited remaining life, and spiritual care coordination with hospice teams.

36 interviews Hour-long each Transcribed + embedded Unlimited Sciences dataset Publication anticipated 2027
2
Survey Data · N=100 Practitioner Survey
Psychedelic Death Worker Study · 63 qualitative responses (subset of 100) · Arrington et al.
▾

Structured survey of 100 practitioners with both psychedelic facilitation and end-of-life care experience — the only dataset of its kind globally. Of those 100, 63 provided in-depth qualitative responses; these 63 are a subset of the 100, not an additional cohort. 36 practitioners separately participated in the Zoom interviews that form Layer 1.

Survey covers: frequency and context of EOL psychedelic work, session structures, outcomes observed, challenges encountered, and recommendations for practitioner training. Quantitative data supports Layer 2; qualitative data supplements Layer 1.

N=100 survey respondents N=63 qualitative responses (subset) Arrington et al., forthcoming 2027
3
Course Recordings · 200+ Hours EOLPC Curriculum
Multiple structured live courses per year since 2023 · 700+ unique enrolled students · 7,600+ free event registrations
▾

Recordings and transcripts from EOLPC's structured live training programs: clinical protocol courses, spiritual care courses, facilitator development intensives, and specialized workshops. Multiple courses per year since 2023, with 700+ unique enrolled students and 7,600+ registrations for free online educational events.

Includes facilitator guides, module outlines, case presentations, guest expert sessions (Ben Malcolm, Dr. David Dansky, and others), and Q&A archives. This layer gives the system EOLPC's proprietary instructional voice and clinical framing.

200+ hours course recordings Facilitator guides 700+ enrolled students 7,600+ event registrations Expert guest sessions
4
Clinical Forums · Practitioner Q&A Networks
Ben Malcolm · Dr. David Dansky · YouTube educational content (~8–10 hrs each) · Google Group archives pending permission
▾

Educational content from two of the most respected practitioners working at the intersection of psychedelics and end-of-life care: Ben Malcolm (Spirit Pharmacist) and Dr. David Dansky. Primary source: YouTube-hosted educational video series — approximately 8–10 hours of content each — covering drug interactions, dose considerations, challenging presentations, facilitator safety, and clinical edge cases.

Note: this layer carries the highest confidence-calibration requirement. YouTube content requires transcript extraction, source attribution, and rigorous accuracy review before embedding. Google Group Q&A archives are a planned additional source, pending explicit permission from community administrators — not yet secured.

Ben Malcolm (Spirit Pharmacist) Dr. David Dansky YouTube educational series (~8–10 hrs each) Google Group archives (pending permission) Highest confidence-calibration requirement
5
Legal & Policy Framework · 30+ Jurisdictions
Ray Allen JD · Antithesis Law, Sacramento · EOLPC's own legal counsel · Oregon · Canada · Europe
▾

Regulatory and legal analysis across 30+ jurisdictions, curated and reviewed by Ray Allen JD (Antithesis Law, Sacramento) — EOLPC's own legal counsel. Covers therapeutic access frameworks, compassionate-use pathways, decriminalization models, facilitator licensing, and EOL-specific legal considerations.

Key jurisdictions: Oregon Measure 109 (active licensed services), Canada Special Access Program, New Mexico Psilocybin Access Act, Switzerland compassionate access, Netherlands retreat frameworks, and select Latin American and Caribbean jurisdictions. Integrated with Psychedelic Alpha's Worldwide Psychedelic Laws Tracker for ongoing updates.
